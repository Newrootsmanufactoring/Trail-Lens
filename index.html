About

Glen Petchey

I walk the Bruce Trail every morning from my home in Owen Sound. Not for exercise. Not to check it off a list. I walk it because the trail always has something to show me — and I bring a camera to prove it.

Trail Lens is my personal record of those walks. The sections I know by heart. The spots most hikers walk right past. The small animals, hidden caves, named geological features, and the way light hits limestone at 7am in October.

I am also an encaustic wax and oil painter, turning the photographs I take into something you can hang on a wall. The same escarpment, the same Georgian Bay light — held in wax.

This is not a hiking app. It is a journal from someone who takes the trail personally.
